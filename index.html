Quiet room

Stay completely silent and still. Do not adjust your level. Record at least 5 seconds of room tone. Auto-stops at 12 s.

Every number is a property of this capture chain, in this room, now, in uncalibrated dBFS. It says whether a take would clear the platform's gates, not how a recording will score with a listener or a model.
