RecQC Standalone

Design stage. The app is not built yet; this page is a placeholder.

A recording-chain and room check that will run in your browser, on your machine, with nothing uploaded: a level check, a quiet-room measurement and a clap-based room-reflection measurement, scored under the MAD collection platform's published policy and reported as the audio tier ceiling a take could earn.
