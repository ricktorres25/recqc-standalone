Quiet room

Stay completely silent and still. Do not adjust your level. Record at least 5 seconds of room tone. Auto-stops at 12 s.

Room reflection

Do not adjust your level. Take 3 steps back from the mic. Clap with cupped hands each time CLAP NOW shows (it hides while the peak-hold line on the meter is pinned by your last clap), and keep going until the recording stops on its own (usually around 6 claps). Aim inside the shaded band on the meter: too hard can distort the reading, too soft may not register, though a few outside it is fine.

Tier ceiling

Record a level take to start.

The environment cap always applies here. On the platform it is a deploy setting (env_tier_cap_enabled), on by default.

Every number is a property of this capture chain, in this room, now, in uncalibrated dBFS. It says whether a take would clear the platform's gates, not how a recording will score with a listener or a model.
